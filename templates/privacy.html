Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our personal expense tracking application (the "Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account. We also collect the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may automatically collect basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, to help operate the Service.

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to provide and maintain the Service, including authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating spending summaries and category breakdowns. We may also use your email address to send account-related communications, such as password resets. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in a database associated with your account and are retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, but no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to operate the Service; these providers only process data as necessary to deliver their services to us and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the support channels listed within the app.
